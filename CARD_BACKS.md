# Pathos card backs

Generated with the built-in image generator. The five separate source images were retained and converted to WebP for the app.

## Common prompt

Create ONE finished portrait tarot CARD BACK texture for a cinematic dark fantasy fortune-telling app. A flat orthographic full-bleed 2:3 portrait image, precisely rectangular, no perspective, no table, no hands, no scene around it. Rich nearly-black jewel-coloured background, an intricate symmetrical antiqued gold double-line border and illuminated manuscript filigree, restrained tiny celestial gold stars, central large instantly recognisable heraldic emblem. Gilded ink on fine textured leather, beautifully crafted luxurious occult art, delicate detail but clear at thumbnail size. Keep border fully inside image with consistent inset margin. No letters, no words, no numbers, no writing, no watermark, no skulls, no character portraits, no hidden lore. Fill entire canvas with the card artwork.
